We are getting ready for our [first IndieWeb meetup](/events/) at Ballarat Hackerspace on Wednesday 26 August and turning this repository into a useful object lesson.

## Making

- A build-time webring that works on any static host.
- Resource pages that are short enough to read during a meetup.
- A visual theme with personality but no external font, image or script dependency.

## Learning

- Which parts of Eleventy make sense fastest when taught in person.
- How much IndieWeb vocabulary is useful before it becomes alphabet soup.
- How to make “view source” feel like an invitation.

## Hoping to meet

People with half-built home pages, elaborate blogs, one-file websites, hand-coded experiments, ancient domains, brand-new domains, and no domain yet.
